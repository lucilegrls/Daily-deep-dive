# Daily Deep Dive — marketing

_19 September 2026. Only factual claims. No invented users, testimonials,
prices or review claims._

## Positioning

A calm, tactile library of deep dives for people who want to understand how
things actually work — and then go to the real sources.

## Audience

Curious adults who read past headlines: students, self-teachers, people in
health, science, law, finance and policy who want the mechanism rather than a
summary.

## Value proposition

- Each topic gives the mechanism, what the evidence supports and what is still
  contested.
- Every source is listed with what was checked and when, so the reader can judge
  it.
- Never Wikipedia; books, papers, videos and articles only.
- Free to browse without an account.

## Features that exist today

Endless personalised feed, search and subject filters across the whole library,
saved topics (in the cloud once signed in), private notes, daily streak, weekly
and all-time ranking, seven visual looks, shareable topic pages.

## Claims we must not make

"Every resource is human reviewed", "hand checked", any user count, testimonial,
price or ranking claim. Where a check was metadata-only, the topic says so.

## Launch readiness

- Ready: reading experience, accounts, personalisation, ranking, sharing.
- Not ready: video coverage (1 topic of 64), the daily publishing rhythm (the
  pipeline exists; no scheduled job has run yet).

## Channel experiments to consider (none run)

Communities built around depth (specialist subreddits, study and medicine
forums), a short "one mechanism a week" newsletter drawn from published topics,
shareable topic pages as the unit of spread, and collaborations with the kind of
expert explainers the editorial policy already favours.
