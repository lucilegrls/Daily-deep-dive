# Daily Deep Dive — Expandable Editorial Topic Map

_Last updated: 17 September 2026_

This file turns broad user ideas into **families of rabbit holes**. It is intentionally not a fixed list. Every family should keep branching as better questions, research and current events appear.

## Editorial rule

A raw label such as `muscles`, `animals`, `neuroscience` or `chocolate` is **not** a publishable topic by itself. Refine it into a question that gives the reader a mental model, exposes uncertainty, and has enough credible evidence to support a real deep dive.

Each published card should ideally answer:

1. What is the mechanism or system?
2. What do we know with relatively high confidence?
3. What is uncertain, contested, conditional or commonly misunderstood?
4. What evidence would change the conclusion?
5. Where should the reader go next?

Wikipedia remains excluded as a resource.

## Expansion principle

These branches are **starting directions, not quotas**. New topics should continuously emerge from:

- current events
- landmark experiments
- new reviews and meta-analyses
- major historical events
- scientific mechanisms
- legal disputes
- economic systems
- technologies
- biological processes
- user curiosity
- contradictions between popular belief and evidence

A good new topic should make the reader think: **“I thought I knew what this was, but I had never seen the system behind it.”**
